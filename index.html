The problem

You started a club to coach kids.
Now you also run an office.

  • RegistrationsCalls, forms and spreadsheets for every new term.
  • Chasing paymentsAwkward reminders to parents you see every week.
  • ContractsPaper to print, sign, scan and file.
  • MessagesThe same parent questions, late at night.

We know it first-hand. We run Robotics Academy, and the paperwork almost ran us.

Kris explains it.

The solution

One system for the whole admin loop.

  1. Online registrationParents pick a group and sign up from your website. Spots update live.
  2. Payments & invoicesInvoices go out on their own. Reminders too, so you don't have to ask.
  3. E-signed contractsParents sign online. Every contract is stored with the child's record.
  4. AttendanceCoaches tick who came, on their phone, in seconds.
  5. Parent portalSchedules, invoices and contracts in one place, so fewer questions reach you.
